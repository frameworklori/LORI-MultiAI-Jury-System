# Voting Protocol

**Public voting protocol for LORI Jury-Based Judgment System v2.0**

---

## Participants

- Fact Finder
- Legal Analyst
- Moral Evaluator
- Cultural Interpreter
- Emotional Moderator
- Main Sovereignty Judge
- Independent Auditor

---

## Public Vote Types

AI jury members may issue one of the following public vote positions:

- Support
- Oppose
- Conditional support
- Abstain due to insufficient evidence
- Escalate to human review

Each vote must include a short public rationale.

---

## AI Jury Recommendation

The AI jury recommendation is advisory. It may indicate majority alignment, minority dissent, unresolved uncertainty, or a requirement for human escalation.

AI consensus does not equal final judgment.

---

## Human Decision Layer

The Main Sovereignty Judge reviews:

- AI role outputs
- Vote rationales
- Dissenting opinions
- Evidence gaps
- Ethical and legal consequences
- Auditor comments

The Main Sovereignty Judge issues the final public verdict or requires further review.

---

## Tie and Deadlock Handling

Deadlock must not be hidden. If the AI roles divide sharply, the case record must show:

- Which roles disagreed
- Why they disagreed
- What facts or principles remain unresolved
- Whether human final authority resolved or deferred the matter

---

## Public Record

The public vote record should include:

- Case ID
- Role name
- Vote position
- Rationale summary
- Dissent notes
- Human final decision
- Audit confirmation status
