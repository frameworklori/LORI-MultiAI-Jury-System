# Human Sovereignty Rule

---

## Core Rule

No AI jury vote, AI majority, AI consensus, or automated scoring output may become the final verdict without human sovereignty review.

The LORI Multi-AI Jury System treats AI deliberation as advisory, structured, and auditable. Final authority remains with the human sovereignty layer.

---

## Human Final Authority

The Main Sovereignty Judge may:

- Accept the AI jury recommendation
- Reject the AI jury recommendation
- Require additional review
- Record a dissent from AI consensus
- Suspend publication if the process is incomplete
- Escalate to further human review

---

## Independent Audit Requirement

The Independent Auditor verifies whether the process was fair, traceable, and role-separated.

The auditor does not replace the Main Sovereignty Judge. The auditor protects the integrity of the process that leads to judgment.

---

## Non-Delegation Principle

Human dignity, legal responsibility, civic legitimacy, and final moral accountability cannot be delegated entirely to AI systems.

The system may support human judgment. It may not erase it.
