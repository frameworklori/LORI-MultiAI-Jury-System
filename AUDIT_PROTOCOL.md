# Audit Protocol

---

## Purpose

The audit protocol protects the integrity of the LORI Multi-AI Jury process.

It verifies that a verdict was produced through traceable role-based deliberation, not through hidden model dominance, emotional manipulation, or unsupported consensus.

---

## Public Audit Questions

The Independent Auditor should confirm:

- Was the case scope clearly defined?
- Were AI roles separated?
- Did each role provide a rationale?
- Were dissenting views recorded?
- Were uncertainty and evidence gaps disclosed?
- Was human sovereignty review completed?
- Was the final verdict clearly distinguished from AI advice?
- Were private implementation details excluded from the public record?

---

## Audit Status Labels

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Audit confirmed | Process appears complete and traceable |
| Audit conditional | Process is usable but has noted limitations |
| Audit incomplete | Missing role output, vote record, or human review |
| Audit blocked | Publication should pause due to integrity concerns |

---

## Public Audit Boundary

The public audit record should describe procedural integrity, not internal security methods.

Private audit memory design, contamination checks, routing logs, scoring boundaries, and manipulation detection internals must remain outside the public repository.
