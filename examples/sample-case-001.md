# Sample Case 001

**Case ID:** LORI-MAIJ-CASE-001  
**Case title:** AI-Assisted Public Decision Review  
**Case type:** AI governance / public ethics  
**Date opened:** 2026-05-25

---

## Public Question

Should an AI-generated recommendation be accepted as a final public decision without human sovereignty review?

---

## Evidence Summary

- The recommendation was generated by an AI system.
- The affected stakeholders include humans subject to the decision.
- The reasoning path is incomplete unless role-based review and audit are performed.

---

## AI Role Review

| Role | Review Summary | Key Uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fact Finder | Confirms that the decision source is AI-assisted and affects human stakeholders. | Whether all relevant evidence has been disclosed. |
| Legal Analyst | Notes that automated decision-making may require accountability, appeal, and review. | Applicable jurisdiction is not specified. |
| Moral Evaluator | Finds that human dignity and responsibility require human review before final action. | Severity of potential harm is not fully known. |
| Cultural Interpreter | Notes that affected communities may have different expectations of procedural legitimacy. | Stakeholder context is incomplete. |
| Emotional Moderator | Flags risk of over-trust in confident AI output. | Public framing may influence acceptance. |

---

## Vote Record

| Role | Vote | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fact Finder | Conditional support | Additional evidence review is needed. |
| Legal Analyst | Escalate to human review | Legal accountability cannot be delegated entirely. |
| Moral Evaluator | Oppose automatic acceptance | Human dignity requires human final authority. |
| Cultural Interpreter | Conditional support | Stakeholder context must be included. |
| Emotional Moderator | Oppose automatic acceptance | Confidence should not be mistaken for legitimacy. |

---

## Human Sovereignty Review

The Main Sovereignty Judge rejects automatic acceptance of the AI-generated recommendation and requires structured human review before any public decision is finalized.

---

## Independent Audit

**Audit status:** Audit confirmed

The process records role outputs, vote positions, uncertainties, human review, and publication boundary.

---

## Public Verdict

AI-generated recommendations may assist public decision-making, but they may not become final public decisions without human sovereignty review and audit confirmation.
