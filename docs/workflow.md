# Public Workflow

---

## Purpose

This workflow describes the public-facing judgment process for the LORI Multi-AI Jury System.

It is a governance workflow, not an implementation manual.

---

## Workflow

1. **Case intake**  
   A case is opened with a public question, case type, evidence summary, and stakeholder map.

2. **Role assignment**  
   AI jury roles are assigned according to function: fact, law, morality, culture, and emotional balance.

3. **Independent role review**  
   Each role produces a bounded review and identifies uncertainty.

4. **Deliberation summary**  
   Points of agreement, disagreement, and unresolved conflict are documented.

5. **Vote record**  
   Each AI role records a vote and public rationale.

6. **Human sovereignty review**  
   The Main Sovereignty Judge reviews the AI outputs and determines whether to accept, modify, reject, defer, or escalate.

7. **Independent audit**  
   The auditor checks process integrity, role separation, and public record completeness.

8. **Verdict publication**  
   The public verdict is published only after human review and audit status are recorded.

---

## Public Boundary

The workflow should disclose what happened at a governance level. It should not disclose private orchestration details, routing logic, scoring internals, or anti-contamination methods.
